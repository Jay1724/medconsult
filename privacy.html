Overview

Our commitment to your privacy

MedConsult (Pty) Ltd is a responsible party as defined in POPIA. We are committed to processing personal information lawfully, with your specific purpose in mind, and only to the extent necessary. This policy applies to all users of the MedConsult platform — doctors, patients, receptionists and practice administrators.

This Privacy Policy describes how MedConsult collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information when you use our practice management platform, including all web portals and services.

By using MedConsult, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of our services and contact us to request deletion of your data.

📋
Who this policy covers
This policy applies to doctors and medical professionals using the Doctor Portal; practice administrators using the Practice Portal; receptionists using the Reception Portal; and patients using the Patient Portal. Different types of personal information are collected from each user category.
Data Collection

What information we collect

We collect only the personal information necessary to provide our services. The information we collect depends on the type of account you hold.

  • Device information (browser type, operating system)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level only)
  • Session data, login timestamps and audit logs
  • Feature usage statistics (anonymised and aggregated)

We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking. We use only essential session cookies required to keep you logged in.

Data Use

How we use your information

We process personal information only for the purposes for which it was collected. These purposes include:

  • Providing, operating and maintaining the MedConsult platform
  • Enabling doctors to access and manage patient records and clinical documents
  • Allowing patients to view their own records, book appointments and receive documents
  • Processing subscription payments and invoicing
  • Generating medical certificates, prescriptions and invoices
  • Sending transactional communications (appointment confirmations, account notices)
  • Ensuring platform security and preventing unauthorised access
  • Complying with legal obligations, including HPCSA record-keeping requirements

Data Security

How we protect your information

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised access, disclosure or destruction.

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Encryption at rest: Database records are encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Role-based access control: Doctors, receptionists and patients each have strictly scoped access — receptionists cannot access clinical records
  • Row-level security: Database-level policies prevent any user from accessing another patient's data
  • Audit logging: All logins, record accesses and document exports are logged with timestamps
  • Authentication: Secure password hashing and session token expiry

In the event of a personal information breach that poses a risk to data subjects, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected data subjects within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by POPIA Section 22.

Data Subject Rights

Your rights under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the following rights in respect of your personal information held by MedConsult:

  • Right to access: Request a copy of all personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Right to restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Right to lodge a complaint: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if you believe your rights have been violated
⚠️
Retention of medical records
Please note that doctors have legal obligations under the National Health Act and HPCSA guidelines to retain patient records for a minimum period (typically 6 years for adults, or until the patient turns 21 for minors). Requests for deletion of medical records may be subject to these retention requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Information Officer using the details below. We will respond to all requests within 30 days.

POPIA

POPIA compliance statement

MedConsult (Pty) Ltd processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and its Regulations. We are registered as a responsible party with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Processing conditions we uphold

  • Accountability: MedConsult takes responsibility for ensuring POPIA compliance across all processing activities
  • Processing limitation: We collect only the minimum information necessary for our stated purposes
  • Purpose specification: Information is collected for specific, explicitly defined and legitimate purposes
  • Further processing limitation: We do not process information in ways incompatible with the original collection purpose
  • Information quality: We take reasonable steps to ensure personal information is complete, accurate and up to date
  • Openness: We maintain this privacy notice and are transparent about our processing activities
  • Security safeguards: We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures
  • Data subject participation: We facilitate the exercise of data subject rights

Special personal information — health data

Patient health information is classified as special personal information under POPIA Section 26 and receives the highest level of protection. We process health data only:

  • With explicit consent of the patient (consent obtained at registration via the treating doctor)
  • Where necessary for medical treatment or the provision of healthcare services
  • Where required to comply with a legal obligation

Cross-border transfers

Our database is hosted by Supabase on servers in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). This constitutes a cross-border transfer of personal information under POPIA Section 72. We ensure adequate protection through contractual measures that provide a level of protection equivalent to POPIA.
