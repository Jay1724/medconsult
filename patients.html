The problem today

Your records stay behind
every time you move on

The South African healthcare system is fragmented. Each practice keeps their own records — and those records are theirs, not yours.

person_off
Starting from zero, every time
Every new doctor asks the same questions from scratch. Your history — conditions, allergies, medications — stays trapped at your last practice.
science
Paying for tests you've already had
Blood results, X-rays, scans — all still sitting at another practice. With no way to share them, you repeat tests you've already paid for.
emergency
Emergencies where no one knows your history
In a crisis, your allergies, chronic conditions and current medication are locked in a folder at a practice you haven't visited in two years.

How it works

No paperwork. No appointments. Ready to use in minutes.

01
Create your record
Sign up free and build your health profile — add your history, chronic conditions, allergies, existing documents and test results.
  • Free for patients, always
  • Takes minutes to set up
  • Upload existing documents and results
02
Share on your terms
Grant a doctor access with a tap. Everything they add during your visit flows straight back into your record — automatically.
  • You approve every practice before they see anything
  • Their notes become part of your record
  • Revoke access any time, instantly
03
Carry it everywhere
Your record lives with you, not with any practice. Take it to any doctor anywhere in South Africa — it's always current, always complete.
  • Works with any MedConsult practice
  • Revoke access and it's gone from their view
  • Your own full file, always visible to you

Built for privacy

Your health information
is yours by law

MedConsult is built around a single principle: your health record belongs to you, not to any practice that treated you.

gavel
POPIA
South African law gives you the right to your health information. The platform is built from the ground up around that right — not as an afterthought.
  • Your data is yours, full stop
  • Export your complete record at any time
  • POPIA compliant data handling throughout
shield
Security
Access permissions are enforced at the database level — not just hidden in the interface. Locked at database level means a doctor whose access is revoked cannot reach your data through any route.
  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access controlled at database level
  • Revocation is immediate and complete
visibility
Transparency
No hidden uses of your data. You can see exactly who has access to your record, what they've added, and when. We don't sell data or show ads — ever.
  • Full visibility of every access grant
  • No data selling, no advertising
  • Audit trail of all activity on your record
