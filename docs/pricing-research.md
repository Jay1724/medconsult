# Pricing research — South African practice management software (Sep 2026)

Prices are from public search snippets and vendor pages. Several vendor sites could not be
opened directly, so verify the numbers before quoting them externally.

## Competitors

| Vendor | Model | Published price |
|---|---|---|
| Bookem | Flat tiers, 21-day trial, no setup fee | Core R 200 · Plus R 650 · Premium R 995 · Medical R 1,495 /mo (up to 3 users, except Medical); extra practitioners R 65–R 500 |
| GoodX | Quote-based | Reported R 450–R 2,500 /mo by number of practitioners; R 1,500 setup fee; 3.4% on online payments |
| Healthbridge | Percentage of monthly claim value | Quote only; add-ons (booking, SMS, telehealth) extra. Acquired the Nora AI scribe in 2026 |
| Elixir Live (Altron), Medinol | Quote-based | Not published |
| Market overall | — | Typically R 500 – R 3,000+ per user per month |

## Claim switching costs (passed on by most vendors)

- Healthbridge: R 5.75 per claim (R 4.10 batched)
- SwitchOn (formerly MediSwitch): from R 5.90 excl. VAT per claim (GP/specialist)

A GP submitting around 400 claims a month pays about R 2,300 in switching fees, which is more than any
MedConsult solo plan. This is why claims are passed through at cost rather than included.

## What this means for MedConsult

- The solo plans (R 299 / R 599) are already far below the market. Keep them as the way in, but
  remove the 50-patient limit, which made Starter unusable for a working GP.
- The old R 5,000 flat Practice price was about double GoodX's reported upper range, and 4× what two
  Professional doctors would pay. It is replaced by per-doctor pricing with a cap.
- Published pricing, no setup fee, no percentage of billings and free migration set MedConsult
  apart from competitors that only give quotes or charge a percentage.
